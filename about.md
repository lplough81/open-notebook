# About this notebook

This is an **open lab notebook**: I record what I do, what I'm thinking, and what happens — including failed experiments and dead ends — and publish it here as I go.

## Why open?

- **Transparency** — methods, raw observations and decisions are visible, not just the polished final result.
- **Reproducibility** — every entry is a plain-text Markdown file under version control, so its full edit history is public on GitHub.
- **Collaboration** — anyone can read along, reuse ideas, or get in touch.

## How entries are organized

Each entry is dated and can carry a **project**, a **status** (e.g. *in progress*, *complete*, *failed*) and **tags**. Browse them by [project]({{ '/projects/' | relative_url }}) or [tag]({{ '/tags/' | relative_url }}), or subscribe via [RSS]({{ '/feed.xml' | relative_url }}).
